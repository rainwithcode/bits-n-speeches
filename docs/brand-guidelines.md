# Brand Guidelines

Adapted from the Toastmasters International Brand Manual for use on the Bits ’N Speeches club website. Website-related rules are drawn from **Protocol 4.0: Intellectual Property**, scoped to what applies at the club level only.

## 1. Colors

### Primary Colors

Three primary colors, one accent. Primary colors may be used for backgrounds and headers; the accent is for highlights only. Black and white are also allowed.

| Color        | Role    | Hex       | RGB           | CMYK            | Pantone |
| ------------ | ------- | --------- | ------------- | --------------- | ------- |
| Loyal Blue   | Primary | `#004165` | 0, 65, 101    | 100, 43, 12, 56 | 302     |
| True Maroon  | Primary | `#772432` | 119, 36, 50   | 12, 95, 59, 54  | 188     |
| Cool Gray    | Primary | `#A9B2B1` | 169, 178, 177 | 23, 7, 12, 18   | 442     |
| Happy Yellow | Accent  | `#F2DF74` | 242, 223, 116 | 0, 5, 57, 0     | 127     |

| Color       | Hex       |

## 2. Typography

- **Gotham** — primary typeface, for headlines and subheads. A Condensed variant is also available.
- **Montserrat** — free alternate, visually similar to Gotham, for use when Gotham isn't available.
- Use standard styles only. No word art, drop shadows, or other manipulation that alters the look of the typeface.

## 3. Photography & Imagery

- Show people who look engaged, empowered, and supported — meetings, presentations, speeches, networking, conferences, training, speech contests.
- Avoid images centered on landscapes, animals, children, food/food appliances, medicine, or architecture — anything disconnected from the Toastmasters environment.
- Cartoons, illustrations, digital art, and clip art are secondary elements only (e.g., meeting themes, seasonal flair) — not primary imagery.
- Never use an image without confirmed permission. Never use trademarked/copyrighted images without written permission from the owner.

## 4. Approved Phrases

Use one phrase per piece of content. Ensure good contrast against the background, and don't let text cover faces or compete with the image.

- Find Your Voice
- Relax, present confidently.
- Relax, speak confidently.
- Communicate Confidently®
- 100 Years of Confident Voices
- Find your confidence
- Become a better leader
- Invest in a Brighter Future

## 5. Club Website Requirements

These are binding protocol requirements for the club website (not just style suggestions).

### Must include

- Club name
- Meeting time and location
- A contact phone number or email address
- The club mission
- A link to the District website
- A link to the official Toastmasters International website
- Dates of latest updates

### Should include

- Event calendar
- Membership benefits
- Performance-motivating articles / newsletters
- Distinguished program goals and progress

### Must NOT include

- Material irrelevant to the club's mission
- Copyrighted material from Toastmasters International or any other source, without written permission from the owner

### Required trademark statement

Every page (or a clearly linked page, e.g. a footer/legal page) must carry this statement verbatim:

> Toastmasters International® owns all Toastmasters trademarks and copyrights, including "Toastmaster," "Toastmasters," "Toastmasters International," and the "official emblem." All club, Area, Division, District, and region websites and social media channels are authorized to use these marks in the form and manner prescribed by the Toastmasters International Board of Directors. Information, photos, and all other materials posted are for the sole use of Toastmasters' members, for Toastmasters business only. It is not to be used for solicitation or distribution of non-Toastmasters material or information.

### Ownership & responsibility

- The **Club President** is the publisher of the club website and is ultimately responsible for all content on it.
- Toastmasters International, as trademark owner, reserves the right to determine how its trademarks are used on the site.

### Hosting

- The club may publish the site on donated hosting space; if so, the donation must be acknowledged on the site.
- No individual member may be compensated to create, maintain, or host the site. Normal, reasonable maintenance costs may be reimbursed by the club.
